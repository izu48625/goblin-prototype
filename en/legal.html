1. Terms of use

What we provide

Create comparison sheets, charts and rankings, and save them as images. Choose Public, Unlisted or Private visibility, and take part in Community ratings. Some features use browser storage or anonymous authentication sessions.

Free features

Current core creation, local saving, image export, publishing and Community features are free. The service has no paid checkout or payment feature. If this changes, any price and conditions will be shown before use.

Acceptable use

Do not publish infringing material, harass others, expose personal information without permission, impersonate others, attempt unauthorized access, spam or manipulate ratings. Only publish material you have the right to use.

Your content

You generally retain the rights to your work. Publishing permits Stats Maker to store, display, share and generate previews of that work as needed to provide the service. Names, designs and software remain the property of their respective rights holders.

Copy & rate (Remix)

When a work offers the copy feature, you may copy its title, description, targets and rating criteria into your own sheet and enter your own scores. The original scores and creator-uploaded target images are not copied; the source-work reference is retained. By publishing a work, its creator permits the copies and displays needed for this feature. This does not grant rights to reuse third-party copyrighted material or trademarks beyond applicable permission or law. Respect others' rights if you publish your copy.

Quality and rights of public works

Ratings are the creator's opinions and are not necessarily official endorsements or objective facts. If a work relies on factual statistics, explain the scope, date and sources where reasonably possible. Do not upload third-party images, substantial copyrighted text or videos without permission or another valid legal basis. When referring to titles or public figures, do not misrepresent your work as an official publication, and do not include personal information or harassing content.

Visibility and backups

Public works may appear in listings and search. Unlisted works are hidden from lists but accessible to anyone with the link. Private works are generally visible only to their creator. Publishing with target images stores reduced-size copies in the cloud. Public work images may be viewed by anyone; Unlisted work images by anyone with the link. Private work images are generally accessible only to their creator. Local data may be lost if browser data is cleared or a device fails; keep backups.

Moderation and availability

We may review, restrict or remove problematic public content. A report does not automatically remove a work. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted service, perfect preservation of data or complete accuracy. Mandatory legal protections still apply.

Service and terms changes

The service may be changed, paused or discontinued. Updates to these terms will be posted here with an updated date and any additional notice required by the change. Use the contact form for questions or moderation appeals.

Governing law and disputes

The service is generally operated under Japanese law. This does not override mandatory consumer protections or other applicable rights in your location. Please contact us first about a dispute; unresolved matters will be handled under applicable law.

2. Privacy policy

Operator and requests

Stats Maker is an individually operated web service. Use the form below for inquiries, information about the operator and requests to access, correct or delete personal data. Operator identity/address details and request procedures required by applicable law will be provided after reviewing the request in accordance with that law.

Information collected and how

Drafts and settings are primarily stored in your browser. Publishing saves titles, descriptions, target and score snapshots, visibility settings and creator identifiers to Supabase. When target images are present, reduced-size JPEG images are also stored in Supabase Storage, shared through time-limited links according to work visibility. Changing a work to Private attempts to delete its published image files. Community participation stores an anonymous user identifier, ratings and submission state.

Reports and contact requests store the selected reason/category, message, work URL and reply email when provided, anonymous user identifier and review status. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, connection and security data when traffic reaches the site.

Purposes and access

We use data to provide sharing, Community summaries, support, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Public works display the creator's published rating results and snapshots to visitors. Individual Community participant ratings, reports and support messages are not publicly readable. Community summaries are shown only when their display conditions are met.

Service providers, storage and cookies

We use Cloudflare for delivery and abuse protection, Supabase for works, authentication and contact records, and jsDelivr for essential frontend scripts. Your IP address and browser information may reach these providers during visits, sign-in or script loading, and may be processed outside Japan. Browser storage holds works, settings and authentication sessions. GA4 tracking is currently disabled. Stats Maker does not load Google Analytics tags or send GA4 page-view or interaction events. The site operator may retain the Google Analytics account for possible future use, but website tracking is switched off. Live advertising and associated ad tracking are not enabled; ad layouts are development-only previews.

3. Copyright, takedown & appeals

To raise a rights complaint, use “Report this work” on a Public work or this form. Include the work URL, your connection to the rights, what content you believe infringes, an explanation of the rights, and a reply email. File attachments are not supported; do not upload identity documents or other sensitive proof into the message field.

We review requests and may seek more information or consider restricting content. To appeal removal or restriction, select “Moderation appeal” and provide the affected URL and reasons. We do not automatically delete content following an allegation or guarantee a response deadline.

4. Private contact form

Use this form for help, privacy requests, copyright notices and moderation appeals. Messages are not public. A reply email is optional for general questions but required for copyright claims, moderation appeals and privacy requests.

Submitting uses an anonymous session. Messages are saved to a private review queue, not posted publicly. Submission does not send an automatic notification email to the operator. Do not enter identity documents, passwords or other sensitive credentials. A reply time cannot be guaranteed.

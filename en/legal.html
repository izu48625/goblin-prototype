2. Privacy policy

Information handled

Drafts and settings are primarily stored in your browser. Publishing saves titles, descriptions, target and score snapshots, visibility settings and creator identifiers to Supabase. Community participation stores an anonymous user identifier, ratings and submission state.

Reports and contact requests store the selected reason/category, message, relevant work URL, optional reply email, anonymous user identifier and review status. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, connection and security data when traffic reaches the site.

Purposes and access

We use this information to create and display works, aggregate Community results, respond to inquiries, review rights complaints, limit abuse and investigate failures. Public works are viewable by others; individual ratings, reports and support messages are not publicly readable. Community averages are shown only when their disclosure conditions are met.

Service providers and international processing

We use Cloudflare for delivery and traffic protection, Supabase for storage/authentication and jsDelivr for certain frontend scripts. Processing may take place outside Japan. Google Analytics 4 is currently disabled and will not be activated without an opt-in choice. Real ads are not currently enabled; any ad layouts are previews.

Retention and your options

You can manage browser-stored content locally. Cloud records and messages are retained while needed for service delivery, support and abuse handling, with deletion reviewed when no longer needed; there is no promised uniform retention deadline. Request access, correction or deletion using the form below.

Analytics choice

If analytics are enabled in future, you will be offered an accept/decline choice before the analytics script loads. Declining will not prevent core use of the site.

3. Copyright, takedown & appeals

To raise a rights complaint, use “Report this work” on a Public work or this form. Include the work URL, your connection to the rights, what content you believe infringes, an explanation of the rights, and a reply email. File attachments are not supported; do not upload identity documents or other sensitive proof into the message field.

We review requests and may seek more information or consider restricting content. To appeal removal or restriction, select “Moderation appeal” and provide the affected URL and reasons. We do not automatically delete content following an allegation or guarantee a response deadline.

4. Private contact form

Use this form for help, privacy requests, copyright notices and moderation appeals. Messages are not public. A reply email is required if you need a response or are submitting a rights/privacy request.

Submitting uses an anonymous session. Messages are private and retained for handling and abuse prevention. Immediate responses cannot be guaranteed.

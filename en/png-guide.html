PNG export guide

Export and share your chart as a PNG

Saving an image for a social post is different from preserving an editable project. This guide explains what to check before exporting and where your PNG may be saved.

Create a chartChoose a chart

Export a PNG in five steps

  1. Enter your data in the basic rating sheet: subjects, criteria and scores.
  2. Pick a visual tool such as Bar Chart, Radar Chart, Tier List or Ranking Card. Read the chart selection guide if you are unsure.
  3. Check your preview for spelling mistakes, cropped labels, numbers and the right to reuse any images.
  4. Export a PNG using the image export or share control in the chart editor. Your device may offer a download or system share action.
  5. Locate the file in your device's downloads, file manager or share destination and open it to make sure it is complete.

If you cannot find the image on your phone

  • On iPhone Safari, a PNG will not always appear automatically in Photos. Check the share sheet and the Downloads folder in the Files app.
  • If labels look cut off, shorten the names or adjust the chart layout before exporting again.
  • If another app makes the result blurry, check whether that destination compressed or cropped the image.

Menus differ by browser and operating system, so confirm the final save flow on your own device.

Use the project backup option separately when you want to preserve your editing data. A PNG alone cannot restore your original editable scores.

Check before sharing publicly

  • Make clear whether rankings are personal opinions or sourced factual statistics.
  • Check the permission to use third-party photos, art, text and logos.
  • Understand the difference between Public, Unlisted and Private works before posting a URL online.

Frequently asked questions

Does exporting a PNG automatically publish a work?

No. Exporting an image and publishing a work are separate actions.

Can I change scores by editing the PNG?

A PNG is a picture, not the original rating data. Use your browser data or a project backup to make further changes.

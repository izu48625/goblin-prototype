How to make a tier list with Stats Maker

  1. 1. Open the free editor

    Use the Create a Tier List button above. The editor starts with editable sample items and S–D tiers, so you can replace the placeholder names and scores immediately.

  2. 2. Choose automatic or manual tiers

    Edit item names and scores in DATA. Automatic mode uses score ranges; manual mode lets you assign each item to a tier. Tier names and score thresholds can be changed.

  3. 3. Export a PNG

    Once the layout looks right, use Share / Save to export an image. Drafts remain in your browser without publishing anything. Public sharing is a separate action.

Tier list vs. ranking: which should you use?

A ranking puts subjects in a strict order: first, second, third. A tier list puts roughly comparable subjects into groups. Suppose two football players have similar overall scores but different strengths in passing and finishing: a tier chart can place them together, while the underlying ratings explain the trade-off. Always make your criteria and subjective judgments clear.

Why build tiers from scores?

Unlike image-only tier boards, Stats Maker lets you establish rating criteria before placing items into tiers. You can use the same ratings for radar charts and ranking cards to show why a subject landed in a given tier. Label personal opinions as subjective, cite factual data, and avoid publishing third-party copyrighted character art or photos without permission or another legal basis.

Tier list maker FAQ

What is a tier list?

A tier list groups subjects into levels such as S, A, B, C and D. It helps compare strength, preference or overall usefulness without forcing every subject into a strict numerical ranking.

Is this tier list maker really free?

Basic tier chart creation, local editing and PNG export are free without an account. Clearing your browser's storage may remove locally saved projects, so keep a backup when needed.

Can I make a tier list without numeric scores?

Yes. Switch the editor to manual tier placement and select a group for each item. Automatic mode is useful when you already have consistent scores.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The editor works in mobile browsers; use Fit to show the entire artwork and Share / Save to export it.

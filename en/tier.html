What Is a Tier? Meaning, Rankings & Tier Lists

A tier is a layer or level in a larger structure. In gaming and rankings, a tier groups people, characters, items or strategies with roughly similar performance or value. That is why a tier list can compare many subjects without forcing every one into an exact rank.

Tier vs. tier list: what is the difference?

The word tier refers to one level or category. For example, a top-tier character belongs to one of the strongest groups under a particular set of criteria.

A tier list is the whole arrangement of those groups, often labeled S, A, B, C and D. The term tier can also describe non-gaming hierarchies, such as seating levels, service plans, or different levels of support.

What do S, A, B, C and D tiers mean?

TierTypical interpretation
STop group: outstanding under the chosen criteria
AStrong group: excellent in many situations
BMiddle group: useful or competitive with trade-offs
CLower group: more limited or situational
DLowest group in this particular comparison

S–D is a common convention, not a universal standard. Some creators add SS, S+, F or other groups. A personal-favorites tier list measures taste, while a competitive game tier list may judge performance in a current ruleset.

Is Tier 1 higher than S tier?

There is no universal conversion. In some competitive rankings, Tier 1 is the strongest group. In progression systems, Tier 1 can instead be the starting equipment level and Tier 3 an upgrade. Letter grades such as S and numerical tiers like Tier 1 must be interpreted using the original game's or author's rules.

Worked example: turn scores into tiers

These six fictional teams were rated out of 100 on Attack, Defense, Speed and Teamwork. All four metrics have equal weight, so a simple arithmetic average determines each group's tier. The scores are original teaching data, not real team performance.

Green Team92.5S
Purple Team83.75A
Red Team77.5B
Blue Team75.0B
Yellow Team60.0C
Gray Team47.5D

For Green Team, (95 + 90 + 95 + 90) ÷ 4 = 92.5, so it enters S. Purple Team averages 83.75 and enters A. Red and Blue have different averages but both belong to B: sharing a tier does not prove they are tied.

Tier lists vs. rankings

A ranking makes a precise order, such as first, second, third. A tier list emphasizes approximate groups that may contain multiple subjects.

For example, weapons can be compared using damage, ease of use, and availability. Two weapons may end up in the same tier for different reasons. In sports, remember that different positions can require different scoring criteria.

Frequently asked questions

Is a tier ranking official?

Not necessarily. Some rankings come from game publishers or tournament organizers, while others express an individual player's opinions. Check the author, criteria and date.

Does Tier 0 or SS always beat S?

Only if the creator defines it that way. There is no worldwide order in which Tier 0, Tier 1, SS and S always correspond.

Is a tier list the same as a ranking list?

Both compare subjects, but tier lists group roughly equivalent items whereas strict rankings put each item in order. The word tier alone has uses beyond games.
